Frequently Asked Questions

What type of infrared sauna is this?

We are pleased to offer a Jacuzzi® brand Clearlight Infrared® Sauna at our clinic. Infrared saunas promise clinically proven health benefits related to detoxification, metabolism, healthy skin, and more. The advanced technology of the Clearlight Infrared® Sauna provides the healthy benefits of sunlight without the harmful effects of UV rays.

How do I know if I should have chromotherapy or red light therapy added?

Chromotherapy and red light therapy are options to augment and enhance the benefits of your infrared sauna therapy. Different colors of lights from chromotherapy each have their specific health benefits, while red light therapy offers added support to reduce inflammation and pain. Our doctors and staff will meet with you to discuss your options and which therapies will be most beneficial for you.

Are there any risks of infrared sauna therapy?

Because sauna therapy promotes sweating, it’s important that you stay hydrated to avoid dehydration. Also, sauna therapy is not appropriate for anybody who cannot tolerate heat. Our clinicians and staff will encourage you to drink water before, during, and after your sauna session and check on you throughout your therapy.

Who is a good fit for infrared sauna therapy?

Anybody who can benefit from supporting their own body’s inherent detoxification systems is a good fit for infrared sauna therapy. This includes people with chronic inflammation or pain as well as individuals looking to optimize and maintain their health.

Do I need to have a membership to book an infrared sauna session?

No. You do not need a membership to enjoy infrared sauna therapy. However, if you do have one of our memberships, sauna therapy is included as one of your plan benefits.
