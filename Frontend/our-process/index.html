Step #3: Lifestyle Guidance

Helping You Create Healthy Changes

Nutrition

We understand that “food is medicine” and an important part of the healing process. We have tailored individualized wellness plans to help guide nutrition, healthy weight loss and lifestyle changes day by day.

Physical Activities

Our Wellness Plans include fitness plans to fit your unique activity level. Our staff is certified in personal training and in yoga instruction to further guide your physical activity goals.

Environmental Toxins

We understand that your home and work environment greatly affect your wellbeing. We help to investigate harmful exposures (Cleaning Products, Mold, Body Sprays, Toiletries, Smoke, Makeup & more) that could contribute to symptoms and prevent complete recovery.

Step #4: Effective Therapies

Detox, Rejuvenate & Heal

IV Therapy

Our intravenous infusions of nutrients can help support the immune system and organs of detoxification to help recover from chronic and acute conditions, or to simply give you a boost!

Frequency Specific Microcurrent™ (FSM) Therapy

FSM is a new system of treatment using micro amperage current and the resonance effects of frequencies on tissues and conditions to create beneficial changes in acute and chronic conditions and in decreasing pain.

Craniosacral Therapy

Craniosacral Therapy (CST) is a gentle, hands-on approach that releases restrictions in soft tissue and fascia that surrounds the central nervous system, joints, organs, muscles, and blood vessels to relieve pain and dysfunction.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber Therapy

A non-invasive therapy that increases the amount of oxygen in your plasma to stimulate tissue healing and fight infections.

Ion Foot Detox

The IonCleanse® detoxification system can reduce symptoms of “toxin overload” from an accumulation of everyday exposures through a series of short detox sessions.

Essential Oil Consultations

Our essential oil consultant can help guide you through the many benefits of essential oils, blends, and oil-infused products that can be used to support physical & mental health concerns and even to safely clean homes and products.
