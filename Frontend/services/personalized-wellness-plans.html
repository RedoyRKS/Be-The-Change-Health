MEDICAL WEIGHTLOSS

View Wellness Plan

The Clean28 Intermittent Fasting Weight Loss Program is a practical, healthy and safe way to lose weight. It is all about eating healthy foods: lean meats, lots of fruits and vegetables, low carbs, and only fats that are healthy such as avocados, olive oil and nuts. This low-carb, moderate protein, moderate fat diet will help you to lose weight fast and to apply these same principles, for life. Carbs never go over 20% per day. Combines these prinicples with Intermittent Fasting (IF).

KETO PLAN

View Keto Plan

A ketogenic diet can lead to significant weight loss by promoting fat burning while reducing carbohydrate intake, which encourages the body to enter a state of ketosis. Additionally, it may improve energy levels and mental clarity, as the brain uses ketones from fat as a more efficient fuel source compared to glucose.
