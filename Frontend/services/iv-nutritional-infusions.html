THERAPEUTIC IVS

PHOSPHATIDYLCHOLINE

Phosphatidylcholine (PC) is a phospholipid found in every cell in our body. It is one of the main components that make up our cell membranes and plays a major role in communication between cells. Phosphatidylcholine is a major supplier of choline and acetylcholine, an excitatory neurotransmitter essential for proper muscle, brain and nerve function. PC is also found in the surfactant in our lungs, allowing us to breathe, as well as, the mucus lining in our gut, allowing us to absorb and digest nutrients.

GLUTATHIONE

Glutathione (GSH) is the “master antioxidant” made by our bodies. Glutathione helps to neutralize and detoxify harmful free radicals, including toxic drugs and heavy metals. By reducing reactive oxygen species, glutathione can protect the brain, kidneys, gut, lungs, and liver from oxidative damage.
