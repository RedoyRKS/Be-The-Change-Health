What is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) is a medical treatment that involves breathing pure oxygen in a pressurized chamber, which allows the lungs to take in more oxygen than under normal atmospheric conditions. This increased oxygen concentration significantly enhances the body’s ability to heal and fight infections, making it beneficial for conditions such as decompression sickness, carbon monoxide poisoning, chronic non-healing wounds, and certain infections.
